In this trailer for Season Five, podcast host Yamini Ranjan discusses the significance of local governance, and the importance of women’s involvement in local governance. She will outline the structure of government in Wayland, the roles of various boards, and the community’s support for the podcast in the episodes. The season aims to explore the challenges women face in local governance and encourage more participation.
